ब्रह्माण्ड

Brahmanda

The universe is an egg wrapped in eight shells: earth, water, fire, air, space, ego, intellect and primordial matter. Each Brahmanda holds its own Brahma, and there are endlessly many of them.

Beyond the egg

    The fourteen worlds, top to bottom

      One day of Brahma lasts about 4.32 billion years, and the lower worlds dissolve at its end. The Puranas, including the Bhagavata, Vishnu and Vayu, differ on distances and on the order of some realms, so this is one common model, drawn schematically and not to scale.
